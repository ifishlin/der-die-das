EP1-new 場四重畫：實驗室牆面新寫法

目的：驗證新的牆面寫法——不能出現第二面側牆、牆上的東西不能掉。04 原 prompt 只改：(1) 附圖換成鏡頭看得到的那幾面牆；(2) 開頭加一句「as in the attached images」並寫檔名；(3) 拿掉畫風句和 Background 文字描述。另外：所有參考圖在 prompt 裡寫檔名；每格附 REF_PROPS；博士和 HERO 同框附 PAIR2_PROF。景別、動作、相機照原本。

畫圖日期：2026-11-24　Flow 專案：EP1-NEW

附的參考圖：

Flow 畫的 4 張（點圖開 Flow）

英文 prompt（送出的原文）

16:9, generate 1 image. The lab's time machine wall and its left side wall, as in the attached images LAB_MACHINE_L and LAB_MACHINE_F2. No text anywhere in the picture. Any props in the picture (the phone, the taped grey box with the red button, the camera, the survival guide) look exactly as in the attached props sheet REF_PROPS. Only two characters in the picture: PROF (exactly as in the attached PROF character sheet REF_PROF) and HERO (exactly as in the attached HERO character sheet REF_HERO). PROF stands on the left, both arms thrown wide in excitement, beaming with a huge open-mouthed grin, mid-announcement. HERO stands closer to the camera on the right, wearing his hat and his backpack, hunched over his phone held in both hands, head bowed, face blank and uninterested. HERO's face and hands are pure paper white. The attached chart PAIR2_PROF shows how tall PROF and HERO are compared with each other; draw them at exactly that height ratio. Shot size: Wide Shot (WS). PROF's whole body is clearly visible, with a lot of the surroundings around him. Camera direction: front three-quarter view. The camera is ahead of PROF and off to one side, so we see his face and one side of his body. Camera angle: Eye-level: the camera is at PROF's eye height, looking straight ahead.
